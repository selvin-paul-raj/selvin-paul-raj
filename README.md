## 🧩 What I build

**At Zinnov (Draup)** · Associate Data Analyst, AI Automation · Sep 2025 to now (Tech Intern, Jun to Sep 2025)

- 🕸️ A **LangGraph multi-agent system** that turns plain-English data requests into validated queries and Excel reports, taking analyst turnaround from hours to under a minute.
- 🔎 **Hybrid retrieval (FAISS + BM25)** across 2.6M company records, plus an entity-resolution agent over 2.4M+ companies and subsidiaries with sub-second lookups and human-in-the-loop checks.
- 📡 A **business-signal monitoring agent** for 537 enterprise accounts across 8 industries: scraping, de-duplication, LLM classification, scoring and automated reports.
